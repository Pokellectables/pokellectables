FAQ

How fast do you ship?
Orders placed before 1pm AEST are dispatched same day. Standard AUS delivery is 1–5 business days depending on location. Express options available at checkout.
How are singles graded?
Every single card is hand-assessed before listing. We use industry-standard condition grades: NM (Near Mint), LP (Light Play), MP (Moderate Play). What you read is what arrives.
Is checkout secure?
Yes. All payments are processed by Stripe — one of the world's most trusted payment platforms. We never store your card details. Supports Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
What's your returns policy?
All sales are final — we do not accept returns. The only exception is items that arrive genuinely damaged in transit, which may be eligible for compensation through the insured value of the shipment via our shipping provider.

To claim for transit damage, a continuous unboxing video is mandatory. The recording must begin before the shipping box is opened and capture the entire unboxing without cuts or interruptions. Any claim submitted with footage that starts after the outer box has already been opened will be automatically rejected, no exceptions.

We strongly recommend recording every unboxing as a matter of habit. If you have a valid damage claim, contact us with your order number and full unedited video footage and we'll take it from there.
Do you do pre-orders?
Sometimes! Pre-orders will be clearly labelled with an estimated ship date. Payment is taken upfront and you'll receive a tracking number as soon as stock lands.
How are cards packaged?
Singles ship in penny sleeves inside rigid toploaders or card savers, then into a bubble mailer. Boxes and tins are wrapped in bubble wrap and shipped in appropriately sized satchels or boxes.
